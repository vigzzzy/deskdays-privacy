🔒 Privacy Policy

Data storage

All data you enter or that is automatically detected — including office attendance records, office locations, and settings — is stored exclusively on your device using SQLite. Nothing is ever transmitted to a server, uploaded to the cloud, or shared with any third party.

Location data

Pyjama Counter requests background location access solely to detect when you are near your configured office locations. Location data is processed on-device in real time and is never stored beyond what is needed to detect arrival and departure. The app only checks proximity to the office coordinates you have explicitly configured — it does not track your movements or record a location history.

Notifications

Notifications are used to confirm office arrivals and deliver optional weekly attendance summaries. No notification content is sent to any server.

Data export

You can export your attendance data as a CSV at any time from Settings. This file stays on your device unless you choose to share it. You can delete all data at any time from Settings → Developer → Clear all data.

Third-party services

Pyjama Counter does not use any analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or third-party tracking services. There are no SDKs that collect data about you.
